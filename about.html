Meet Our Founder – Shekar

Behind every delicious meal at Annam Restaurant is the passion and dedication of its founder, Mr. Shekar. His journey began with a deep appreciation for the rich culinary traditions of South India and a dream to share the authentic taste of homemade food with everyone. Inspired by family recipes that have been passed down through generations, he believed that every meal should be prepared with love, care, and the finest ingredients.

With this vision, Mr. Shekar established Annam Restaurant to bring people closer to the comforting flavors of traditional South Indian cuisine. His goal was not just to serve food but to create an experience that reminds customers of the warmth, hospitality, and happiness of a home-cooked meal. Every dish is carefully crafted using fresh ingredients, traditional spices, and time-tested cooking techniques to preserve its authentic taste and aroma.
